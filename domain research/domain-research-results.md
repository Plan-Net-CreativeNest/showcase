## ⚡ The moodmode.com Opportunity

**moodmode.com** is registered but expiring around **June 2026** — roughly a month away from the research date. If this name resonates with your brand vision, act fast.

**How to pursue it:**

1. **Set a backorder** — [Namecheap Backorder](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/backorder/) or GoDaddy Backorder (~$10–20). They queue automatically to grab it the moment it drops.
2. **Drop-catching services** — [DropCatch](https://www.dropcatch.com) or [SnapNames](https://www.snapnames.com) specialise in catching dropped domains competitively.
3. **Contact the owner directly** — Since the domain appears unused and is clearly expiring, the current owner may sell cheaply before it lapses.
