# Creative Direction — felt.

**Purpose:** Canonical reference for the confirmed brand name, domain, visual direction, tonal identity, and refined onboarding language.  
**Last updated:** 2026-05-28 by Liana

---

## Name

**felt.**

Lowercase. Always with a trailing full stop.

The name is deliberate in its simplicity — a past-tense verb turned into a quiet noun. It evokes emotion as something already experienced, something real and embodied. Not what you *think*, not what you *say* — what you *felt*. The period is part of the mark: it signals a pause, a moment of stillness, an ending that is also an arrival.

## Art Direction

**Tone:** Quiet. Introspective. Grounded. Safe.

**Aesthetic:** Minimalist, clean, texture-driven, earthy, natural soft tones.

**Conceptual frame:** Innerweather — the idea that emotional states are like weather: shifting, cyclical, and beyond judgment. felt. borrows the language of climate and seasons to make feelings feel impermanent and natural rather than fixed or clinical.

**Core keywords:** feel · sensory · private · awareness · purpose · patterns · seasons · change
